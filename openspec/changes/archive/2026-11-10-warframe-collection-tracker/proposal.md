## Why

A personal Warframe collection needs a single place to plan acquisition, crafting and Platinum spending.

## What Changes

- Add a searchable catalog of standard and Prime Warframes with per-component and final ownership.
- Add recipes, farm locations, relic rewards, shared resource counts and a collection dashboard.
- Integrate read-only Warframe.market quotes and optional manually recorded purchase prices.
- Record the target Warframe PC version and provide a catalog refresh with a change report.

## Capabilities

- `collection-tracking`: collection, acquisition, inventory and market budget.
