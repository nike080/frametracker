## Why
FT-22 replaces the inventory table with resource cards so players can update stock after a session with fewer repeated edits. Russian resource names were only partially localized.

## What Changes
- Square resource cards with local images, stock, required quantity and shortfall.
- Quick steps ±1, ±10, ±100 and ±1000; custom additions/subtractions; inline stock editing with save/cancel.
- Complete official Russian resource names, bilingual search, and an instruction above the cards.
- Preserve unknown stock, undo, persistence, goal scope and filters.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- inventory-cards: Resource card presentation and editing, localization and discoverability.

## Impact
Changes the inventory view, catalog resource labels, localization update tools and portable smoke check. Saved IDs and progress format remain compatible; recipes and summary retain their table views.
